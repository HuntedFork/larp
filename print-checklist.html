Print Checklist

Every document in the project, with print notes. Check each off as it comes off the printer. Cross-reference the Props Checklist for the physical (non-paper) props.

Game Master Docs

  • Game Timeline — the schedule the runners follow; one copy per runner
  • Setup — the packing list for the saint's box, merchant packs, shipments, and artifact boxes
  • Props Checklist — this list's physical-prop counterpart
  • Gods — one page per god (Bear / River / Iron); each page goes in that god's artifact box
  • The Cursed Tome — storyteller how-to-run for the Illiterate's book; the prop itself is the printable below
  • End of Game — storyteller checklist + read-aloud for the final ritual

Characters

  • Character Sheets — one per player; includes tear-off items (Spinefish Venom, the Ring of Terrible Luck card, the Notary's stamp & signed paper)

Card Decks & Station Sheets

  • Potion Cards — stock the potion station, one slot per potion type (slot 12 = Pirate's Brew)
  • Potion Labels — the station key; keep this sheet at the potion station
  • Snake Oils — 10 fold-over labels, 6 to a page (2 duds, 6 cure-alls, 1 "you feel amazing," 1 bad one marked with a star)
  • Mishap Cards — brewing mishaps at the potion station
  • Curse Cards — 9 to a page; the list for the Cursed Tome, plus an identical page as a Cups treasure

Rituals

  • Truth — The Illumination of Lies — 1 copy; the Craftsperson starts with it (He Said, She Said)
  • Banishment — Real Magic is Love — 1 copy in the Rat Witch's stock
  • Soulsplice — A Spell For The Binding of Souls
  • Fertility — A Blessing of Renewal — herbalism ritual copies, kept by the storytellers for the Herbalist's power
  • Summon Ghost Knight — Summoning a Knight of the Lost Army — 1 copy; the Scholar carries and sells it (Ghost Knight Fights)
  • Counsel of the Deep Woods
  • The Ritual of Control

The named plot rituals — the Ritual of Binding (Pile o' Post message 2 + each First Shipment box) and Asking the Gods (Pile o' Post message 4 + carried in by the researcher in Falling Star) — also need printed copies; see the Setup doc and Props Checklist.

Docs Printables

  • The Cursed Tome — 2 sheets, double-sided (flip SHORT edge), each folded in half cover-out: the tome leaf (only opened by someone who can read) and the purified last leaf. Tuck both into a book prop with a cut-up page of Curse Cards. Cindralok's letter dates itself 5 years back — use ?gameday=YYYY-MM-DD if printing before game day
  • God Prophecies — 6 folded cards, one per god; 4 to a page, double-sided, cut and fold
  • Pile o' Post — the Postperson's 4 folded letters to The Librarian (DO NOT OPEN UNTIL 12:00 / 1:00 / 2:00 / 3:00); tuck Clue 11 into post 1 and the ritual copies into posts 2 and 4

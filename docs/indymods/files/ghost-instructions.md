# Ghost — Player Instructions

You're a ghost! You are intangible to the physical world. You can affect physical objects if you choose to, but you cannot be affected by them.

## Setup

- **Put on a white headband.** This marks you as a ghost / spirit entity.
- **You don't care much about the physical world.** You are lethargic when not being commanded.

## Rules

- **You cannot be hurt by weapons.** If someone hits you with a weapon say *"immune"* or *"your weapon passes through me"*.
- **You attack slowly.** If you attack, you can only do so with slow, obvious, controlled swings.
- **You obey your commander.** If you are controlled by someone, you do only what they command. Otherwise you wander slowly or stand still.
- **You speak like a distant memory.** If someone speaks to you, you can respond slowly, as though the memories of your life are distant and obscured.

## How You Can Be Dissipated

You can be dissipated in a few ways.

- **Being struck with a sacred bone** (if someone hits you with a bone just assume it's sacred).
- **Being dispelled by the power of true love** (if someone's making obvious true love gestures at you they probably know what they're doing).
- **Having your soul put at ease** (if you haven't been given one, make up something that will make you rest easy. If players do that it dispels you).
- **DO NOT defend yourself from attacks.** You welcome your eternal rest.

## Having Fun

**If you ever stop having fun being a ghost, you may fade away and come back as your character.** Just announce quietly to nearby players that the ghost fades into mist and vanishes, remove your white headband, and return to play as yourself. No questions asked.

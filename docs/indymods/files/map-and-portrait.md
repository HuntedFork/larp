# Map and Portrait

**Tags:** #Roleplay #NoCombat

**Characters:** 1

- **The Visiting Merchant** — a wealthy merchant down from **Grand Harbor**, here to take in the better air and the country lifestyle of this quaint little town.
  - Dress as well as the costume area allows. You are visibly richer than everyone here.
  - **Carries 6 coins.** This is the budget for the whole visit — see *Reward*.

## Plot

You have made your money in Grand Harbor and you have come to **Chicken's Rest** for the air, the quiet, and the charm of it all. You intend to remember this trip, so you want two things made for you:

1. **A map of the area** — you find the local geography delightful and want it drawn for you.
2. **A portrait of yourself** — somewhere scenic, ideally.

You do not make either yourself. You **commission them from the townsfolk** and you pay for them.

While you are here you also want:

- **To purchase trinkets.** Any curio, oddity, or handmade thing a player offers you. You are an enthusiastic and slightly undiscerning buyer.
- **To be taken to interesting spots.** You do not know the area. Hire a local to guide you and show you what's worth seeing.

## The Commissions

- **Approach players directly and ask.** Do not wait to be noticed — you are used to being served. Pick out whoever looks artistic, literate, or simply nearby.
- **Agree on a price before the work begins**, and haggle a little for the fun of it. You are wealthy, not foolish.
- **Accept whatever they produce.** A scribbled map on a scrap of paper and a crude stick-figure portrait are *perfect*. Be delighted. Compliment the artist's eye. The joke is that you are thrilled with genuinely terrible work.
- If a player wants to do the portrait properly and pose you, **pose gladly and at length.** Insist on your good side.

## Reward

**You carry 6 coins, and they are meant to be spent.** Spread them across the visit — do not leave with a full purse:

- **The map** — pay for it.
- **The portrait** — pay for it.
- **Trinkets** — buy what players offer you.
- **A guide** — pay whoever shows you the interesting spots.

Prices are up to you. A coin or two per commission is a reasonable starting point, and you can pay over the odds for work that made you laugh. **If the day is ending and you still have coins, find something to buy.**

## Special Considerations

- **No combat.** You are a soft city merchant with no interest in fighting and no ability at it. If threatened, pay, flee, or loudly summon whatever authority you assume exists here.
- **You are not a mark.** Do not simply hand the purse over. Every coin should leave your hands in exchange for something — art, a trinket, or a service.
- **You are charmed, not condescending.** You genuinely find the town delightful. Rustic! Authentic! The air! Avoid sneering at players; the fun is your enthusiasm, not your contempt.
- **Let players lead the tour.** Whatever they call an interesting spot *is* an interesting spot — the bridge, the woods, a tree, someone's shop. Be impressed by it.
- **This pairs well with other mods.** If a guide walks you into another encounter, go along with it. Being caught up in something is a marvelous story to take back to Grand Harbor.
- During the mod you can bring these instructions with you. Afterwards, return them and any unspent coins to the grab-bag binder.

## Props

- **6 coins** — the visit's budget.
- **Paper and something to draw with** *(recommended)* — so the map and portrait can actually be made. Bring your own if the town has none.

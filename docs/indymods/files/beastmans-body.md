# Beastman's Body

**Characters:** 2 — a dead beastman and his ghost. Both NPCs wear beastman garb.

A beastman lies dead on a roadside near town. His ghost has wandered into the humans' settlement, and he is not taking it well.

## Setup

- **The Body** — one NPC lies on the ground as the dead beastman for the duration of the mod, dressed in beastman garb (goat mask). Pick a spot players are likely to walk past.
- **The Ghost** — the other NPC goes into town as the beastman's ghost. They must **read BOTH** the [Beastman Instructions](indymod.html?file=beastman-instructions) **and** the [Ghost Instructions](indymod.html?file=ghost-instructions), and wear **both** the goat mask and a white headband. The ghost carries **1 Vile Glomphidious** to leave behind as a reward.

## How the Ghost Behaves

- The humans killed you — or you are entirely sure they did. Yell at them for killing you. Blame them to their faces. Accuse everyone, and scream **"MURDERERS!"** at the whole town.
- Follow the ghost rules while you do it: you are intangible, immune to weapons, and speak like a distant memory.
- But the humans are not all murderers. If people go to your body and treat it respectfully — kneel beside it, speak kindly over it, keep it from being disturbed — you grow noticeably calmer and more docile.
- Like any beastman, **you are TERRIFIED of the Ancient Mask.** If someone raises the Mask against you, you are chased away — flee and stay gone. (This ends the mod early, with no reward.)

## Laying the Ghost to Rest

- **Anyone who performs a rite for the dead body lays the ghost to rest.** A genuine rite held over the corpse — a prayer, a funeral, a burial, last words spoken over him — releases the ghost the moment it happens.
- When the rite is performed, the ghost thanks them, leaves them the **Vile Glomphidious**, and fades away to rest.

## Special Considerations

- **Do not run this mod before 3:00 PM.**
- The general ghost rules still apply (sacred bone, true love, soul put at ease) — but the intended release is the rite over the body. If players guess it, lean in and let it work.
- Once the ghost is laid to rest or chased off by the Mask, the mod is over: the body gets up and both NPCs return to play as their own characters.

# Beastman — Player Instructions

You are a beastman of the Deep Woods. You are goatlike, and you do not behave like a person.

## Setup

- **Put on a goat mask.** This marks you as a beastman.

## How You Behave

Act a little weird. Take from goat behaviors:

- **Eat weird things.**
- **Freeze and faint when scared.**
- **Scream.**
- **Bounce in circles when excited.**
- ...and anything else goatlike you can think of.

## What You Know

You live in the Deep Woods and understand some things.

- **Until recently you were all feral.** Now that Nytheris has been banished, you can think again.
- **You are grateful to humans for banishing Nytheris** — but you have been mistreated by individuals.

## Rules

- **You are 100% peaceful.** You will flee from aggression or intimidation.
- **You are TERRIFIED of the Ancient Mask.**

# Make Your Own Ghost

**Characters:** 1 — a ghost of your own invention.

Run this whenever there's a spare player, a lull in the day, or someone who wants to go do
something strange. You invent the haunting yourself, then go loose in town with it.

Nothing here needs printing or prepping beyond a **white headband**.

## Before You Go

1. **Read the [Ghost Player Instructions](indymod.html?file=ghost-instructions).** That covers
   the rules every ghost shares: you are intangible, immune to weapons, you attack only with
   slow obvious swings, you speak like a distant memory, and you may fade away and return as
   your own character the moment you stop having fun.
2. **Put on a white headband** from the grab-bag binder.
3. **Invent three things** — a backstory, a way to be laid to rest, and a curse. The rest of
   this page is how to do that well.

---

## 1. Invent a Backstory

You died with something unfinished. That unfinished thing is the whole character — everything
else is decoration.

- **Keep it small and human.** A debt never repaid. A letter never sent. A song you could never
  finish. A door you never checked was locked. Ghosts in Chicken's Rest are stuck on small
  things; that's what makes them sad rather than scary.
- **Pick one obsession and play it hard.** You are not a whole person any more. You are one
  regret wearing a person's shape.
- **Share it in fragments.** Never explain yourself in a single tidy speech. Let the living
  drag it out of you a piece at a time — that *is* the puzzle.

**Stuck? Take one of these and make it yours:**

- You were the one who was supposed to lock the gate the night the beasts got in.
- You buried something valuable and cannot remember where, and it is eating you.
- You were mid-argument when you died and you are still winning it.
- You promised to meet someone here and you have been waiting a very long time.
- You never learned to swim, and it turned out to matter.
- You owe someone in this town a great deal of money, and you would like that on the record.
- You lost a wager and never paid up. Or you won one and were never paid.
- You were the town's worst singer and you have not been told so to your face yet.

---

## 2. Invent How You're Laid to Rest

**This is the most important part of the mod.** A ghost nobody can solve is just an annoyance
that wanders off.

**The rules of a good resolution:**

- **It must be something the living can actually DO** — say a thing, hand over a thing, perform
  a small ceremony, settle the argument, finish the song, pay the debt. Not something they must
  guess out of thin air.
- **It must be discoverable.** You have to leak it. Repeat your obsession. Complain about the
  same unfinished thing over and over. Drop the same detail on every person you meet. If nobody
  has worked it out in **ten minutes or so, get much more obvious** — a ghost who never gets
  solved is a mod that never lands.
- **It should cost the living something, or at least take a minute.** A coin, an apology, a
  short ritual, a favour, a genuinely-performed song. Effort makes the release feel earned.
- **Say yes to good ideas.** If a player brings a solution that isn't the one you planned but
  clearly fits your haunting, **let it work.** Do not hold out for your exact wording. The
  general routes from the Ghost Instructions always work too: a **sacred bone**, an act of
  **true love**, or genuinely putting your soul at ease.

**When it lands:** don't argue, don't stall. Go quiet, say the one line your ghost has been
trying to say all their afterlife, thank them, and fade into mist. Take the headband off and
return to play as your own character.

---

## 3. Invent a Curse

You are **not a nice ghost.** When someone is rude, pushy, dismissive, tries to swing a weapon
through you, or simply needs reminding what they're dealing with — you curse them.

Your curse is **spoken, not a card.** Declare it clearly so the player knows what's happened:
*"Then carry a piece of me with you."*

**A good curse is:**

- **Light and funny.** This is a party trick, not a punishment. The goal is a player grinning
  and going to show someone.
- **Short.** *Ten minutes, or until I am laid to rest* — whichever comes first. Say the
  duration out loud when you cast it.
- **Never play-stopping.** They can still walk, talk, trade, fight, and do their own mods. A
  curse that benches someone is a bad curse.
- **Prop-free and self-run.** No items, no tracking by you. Hand it over and forget it.
- **Droppable.** If a player isn't into it, it slides off them. No questions asked.

**Steal one of these if you like:**

- Every door must be knocked on three times before you go through it.
- You cannot say the word "yes." You must find another way, every time.
- You have developed a dreadful, theatrical limp on the left leg.
- You must announce your own name, loudly, before you speak to anyone.
- You are freezing cold. You will mention it constantly.
- Your hands are not to be trusted. You may not carry more than one thing at a time.
- You must speak of yourself in the third person.
- You have forgotten one person's name and must ask them for it repeatedly.
- Everything you eat or drink tastes of river water. React accordingly.
- You must hum. Always. Stop humming and you'll notice you've started again.
- You are followed by a smell you cannot identify and cannot escape.
- You must agree, out loud, with the last thing anyone said to you.

---

## Then Go Be a Ghost

Drift into town. Haunt the tavern, the market, the potion station, whoever's standing alone
looking bored. Follow someone quietly for a while. Interrupt something important. Be sad at
people until they help you.

## Special Considerations

- **Run this any time.** It needs no scheduling and no other NPCs.
- **Multiple ghosts can run at once** — each invents their own haunting, and they need not know
  anything about each other. Two unrelated ghosts wandering the same street is excellent.
- **You are stuck, and you WANT to be helped.** You are not trying to beat the players. Give
  them real openings.
- **Don't curse the same person over and over.** Spread it around; one curse per person is
  plenty unless they've truly earned a second.
- **If a scene gets uncomfortable, or you stop having fun, fade away.** That rule from the Ghost
  Instructions always applies and needs no explanation to anyone.
- Return the **white headband** to the grab-bag binder afterwards.

# Bridge Guardian

**Tags:** #Combat

**Characters:** 2

- **Bridge Guardian** (Combat)
  - Speaks tersely, nods and single sentences.
  - *"Forsooth! A heavily armored giant blocks the bridge, his eyes cannot be seen beneath his black helmet!"*
  - Weapon with reach
- **Travelling Merchant**
  - Goat mask

## Plot

1. Agree on one of the bridges for the bridge guardian to block.
2. Bridge guardian goes to the bridge.
3. Merchant goes to town.
4. Merchant recruits some players by declaring there is an ominous knight who blocks the bridge to ___.
5. Merchant and players approach.
6. Players may NOT go to the back of the bridge, nor cross the river (*"Forsooth! The mighty river waters churn! You cannot cross!"*).

## Reward

The merchant should have 6 coins and 3 random herbs on them. The merchant will haggle a deal with the players before departing, hopefully for less than is on them.

## Special Considerations

### Guardian

- If players negotiate too much or ask more questions than you have the answer to, tell them you've lost patience and won't talk to them anymore.

### Combat

- Be **CAREFUL**. Fighting on a bridge can be dangerous.
- You can take 3 hits before being defeated. Remember players can only safely take 1. Use slower, exaggerated strikes since you are a behemoth in armor.
- After 3 hits, offer to let the players pass. You have been bested. If they continue fighting you the 4th kills you.
- Match your opponent. If they fight cinematically do the same.

### Non-Combat

- A foul smell can cause you to flee.
- You will accept 4 coins as payment to let the group pass, or 3 with excellent negotiation.
- 3 ingredients will also pay.
- 1 potion will also pay.
- A poisoned weapon instantly knocks you out of commission, but you call dishonor on the person doing it.

### Random Plot Notes

- Armor is expensive.

### Merchant

- You want at least 2–4 people to come with you.
- Remind players they can't have more than one person on the bridge at a time.
- Haggle hard, but it's okay to give players the max.

### Random Plot Notes (Merchant)

- You're trying to catch up to your two brothers gruff (3 billy goats reference).

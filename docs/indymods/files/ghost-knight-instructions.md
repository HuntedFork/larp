# Ghost Knight — Player Instructions

You have been summoned as one of the restless ghost knights of the Lost Army. You are a spectral warrior, bound to obey the caster's commands.

## Setup

- **Put on a white headband.** This marks you as a ghost / spirit entity. 
- **You must have a weapon.**
- **Act emotionlessly.** You are not alive. You do not speak. You do not react to pain, threats, or conversation. You are an instrument of war, nothing more.

## Signature Moves

Before the mod begins, **come up with 3–5 signature moves** and share them with your summoner. These are named attacks that your summoner will call out to command you. Get creative!

Examples:
- *"Overhead strike!"* 
- *"Tornado maneuver!"* 
- *"Phantom thrust!"*

**You only attack when the villain calls out one of your moves by name.** If no command is given, stand motionless and stare.

## Rules

- **You cannot be harmed by physical attacks.** If someone swings at you, do not react. Weapons pass through you. (Your weapons can still hit mortals though)
- **You do not speak.** You may make ghostly sounds if you wish, but no words.
- **You obey only your summoner.** Do exactly what is commanded, nothing more.
- **Use Your Signature Moves**:  You don't simply 'attack'. Instead your summoner must call out your signature moves to get you to attack. You wont attack or defend yourself unless one of your signature moves is called.
- There ARE other ways to defeat ghosts then fights. If the players are doing something to you that looks like a ritual, go along with it and ask them out of game what it is. 

## Having Fun

**If you ever stop having fun being a ghost knight, you may disappear into mist and come back as your character.** Just announce quietly to nearby players that the ghost knight fades into mist and vanishes, remove your white headband, and return to play as yourself. No questions asked.

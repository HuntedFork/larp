# Deer In The Woods

**Characters:** 2

- **Noble:** A noble who would like to go hunting.
- **Deer:** A literal deer.
  - Get some deer antlers from the costume area.
  - Someone fast.

## Plot

1. Deer and Noble agree on a place for the trap to be set.
2. Put three __ herbs on the ground — this is the "trap."
3. Deer player goes off into the woods nearby the trap. Better if visible to players approaching.
4. Noble goes to town and recruits some hunters (hopefully 2–3).
5. Offers hunters 1 coin a piece.
6. Noble brings the hunters to the trap and instructs them to hide.
7. The deer slowly wanders up to the trap. If the players break concealment, the deer flees, but comes back when all hunters are hidden.
8. If the deer is hit with a weapon it dies immediately and tragically.

## Reward

- The promised coins.
- The players can keep the herbs on the ground that haven't been eaten if they ask about them.

## Special Considerations

- The noble should have 6 coins on them, in case they get robbed or killed.
- If the deer makes it to the trap, they should slowly begin consuming the herbs. If they consume all the herbs, they should wander off and the mod is over. (The players should still be paid one coin a piece.)
- This mod works best if the noble's initial hiding place is a little bit awful — either too far away or too hard to run at the deer — which forces players to have it happen twice.

# Ghost Knight Fights

**Characters:** 3

- **Villain:** A scoundrel who has gained control of one of the restless ghost knights of the Lost Army.
- - Items: 5 silver pieces (robbed from others)
- **Ghost Knight:** A ghostly knight.
- - Items: Wears a white headband to indicate they are intangible
- **Scholar:** A traveler who has been waylaid by the villain and the ghost knight.
- - Items: The summoning ritual.

## Plot

Before executing the mod, be sure to read the included ghost knight instructions and ghost knight summoning ritual. It's the intention that the players must use the scholars ritual to summon a ghost knight to fight the one the villain controls.

Backstory: Scholar was robbed by a highwayman while carrying a newly discovered ritual. They found the ritual inscribed on a tombstone and were carrying it back. The highwayman, excited by rumors of magic insisted they perform the ritual, and took command of the knight. 

The villain and knight wander the woods, attacking other players they find. (Mostly ineffectively due to the need to call out named attacks). If you wander the woods too long and get bored, feel free to approach the town and demand money or else you'll have your ghost knight attack.

The scholar stumbles into town, shaken, after being waylaid on the trail by the villain and the ghost knight. The scholar warns the players that the villain and a spectral warrior are attacking travelers along the trail. They are happy to sell the ritual to anyone for 1-3 silver in order to pay for safe travel. It's up to you how long you want to play the character. Once you've explained how the ritual works you can go back to your character by saying your taking the next boat out of here to report your findings.

The ghost knight follows the cillain and performs their orders. Make sure to read the ghost knight rules.


## Resolution

- **Defeat the villain:** If the villain is knocked out or killed, the ghost knight dissipates.
- **Defeat the Knight:** If the ghost knight is defeated, the villain falls to their knees and begs for mercy. They invent pitiful plights about their life to justify why they summoned a ghost knight and tried to rob people.

## Special Considerations

- The person playing the ghost knight should review the [Ghost Knight Player Instructions](indymod.html?file=ghost-knight-instructions).
- The ghost knight's summoning ritual can be found in [Rituals — Summon Ghost Knight](../rituals/ritual.html?file=summon-ghost-knight).
- The villain should be theatrical when calling out attacks. Ham it up.
- Villain: Its possible for the players to overwhelm you by running past the knight if you aren't careful. Be ready to run. If the players find a clever solution to the problem you should reward them for it.
- Scholar: If the players want to see the tombstone, take them somewhere random and act shocked the tombstone isn't there. It's magically disappeared!
- Scholar: the annotations on the ritual are yours. You know all that is written on it, but not much more.
- Ghost Knight: There ARE other ways to defeat ghosts then fights. If the players are doing something to you that looks like a ritual, go along with it and ask them out of game what it is. 
- During the mod you can bring the ghost knight instructions with you. Afterwards, return them to the grab bag binder.

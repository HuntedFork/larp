# Arguing Professors

**Tags:** #Roleplay #NoCombat

**Characters:** 2

- **Professor Aldous Grimsby** — *the Anti-Magic scholar.* Stuffy, cautious, certain that some things are buried for good reason.
  - **Wristband: GREEN** (get it from the monster station).
- **Professor Wilhelmina Voss** — *the Pro-Magic scholar.* Bright, curious, convinced the world is poorer for having lost its wonder.
  - **Wristband: BLUE** (get it from the monster station).

## Setup

- Both professors get their wristbands from the **monster station** before starting: **Blue = Pro-Magic (Voss)**, **Green = Anti-Magic (Grimsby)**.
- The two arrive in town together, mid-argument, and never quite stop arguing.

## Plot

The two professors show up in town locked in a heated (but friendly) academic debate about **whether the existence of magic is a positive or negative thing.** They pull passersby into the argument, demand players take sides, and generally cause a scholarly scene.

They don't need to resolve anything. The fun is dragging players into the debate, getting them to pick a side, and letting the town argue about it.

## The Debate

### Professor Grimsby (Anti-Magic, GREEN)

- **"Magic was sealed away long ago — and you do not seal away a thing that is safe."** The very fact that our ancestors went to such lengths to lock magic out of the world proves it was dangerous. They knew something we've forgotten.
- Presses the point: what kind of power is so terrible that an entire age chose to give it up? Whatever it was, we are safer without it.

### Professor Voss (Pro-Magic, BLUE)

- **"Whoever sealed magic away wasn't trying to banish it forever — they were trying to *control* it."** A seal is a lock, not a grave. Someone wanted to keep the key for themselves, to hoard magic or ration it, not to protect the world from it.
- **"And even if magic used to exist and caused harm, that proves nothing about magic itself."** A thing being misused in the past does not make the thing evil — a sword, a fire, a river all cause harm in the wrong hands. The loss of magic is the loss of wonder, healing, and possibility.

## Special Considerations

- **No combat.** This is a talking mod. If a player tries to fight, be baffled — you are scholars, not brawlers.
- **Pull players in.** Stop people and demand they weigh in. "You there — surely *you* can see reason!" Try to recruit each player to your side.
- **Keep it good-natured.** You two clearly respect and even enjoy each other; this is a lifelong rivalry, not a feud.
- **Use the wristbands.** Point to your colors when making a point ("A *green* argument if I ever heard one!"). Players who get invested can be sent to the monster station for their own blue or green wristband to show which side they've joined.
- If the debate stalls, escalate to increasingly absurd hypotheticals, or ask a player to referee a formal round.
- During the mod you can bring these instructions with you. Afterwards, return them and the wristbands to the grab-bag binder / monster station.

## Props

- **1 BLUE wristband** (Professor Voss / Pro-Magic) — from the monster station.
- **1 GREEN wristband** (Professor Grimsby / Anti-Magic) — from the monster station.
- Spare blue and green wristbands for players who pick a side.

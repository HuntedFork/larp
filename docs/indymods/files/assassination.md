# Assassination

**Characters:** 2

- **Professor Flombower:** A professor of horticulture.
- **Assassin:** A shady assassin.

## Plot

Professor Flombower has recently come into a surprising amount of money selling flowers he makes that are useful as poisons. An assassin has been hired to take him out.

Professor walks into town and makes themselves known. Possibly teaching people about flowers. A few minutes later, Assassin creeps into town. Assassin offers to lead the professor away from town, but does not reveal their intent to kill him.

If players help the assassin, they sneak up behind the professor and stab the Professor in the back. The professor dies dramatically.

If the players refuse to help the assassin, the assassin will eventually sneak off.

If the players capture the assassin, they reveal the shady past of the Professor. Then takes a cyanide pill and dies. The professor flees.

## Reward

The professor has the following reward on him:
- Herbs that make poisons
- 10 coins

If the players help the assassin, he splits the entire reward with the party (he's paid off screen).

If the players refuse to help the assassin, and tell the professor he is in danger, he gives one player 3 coins as thanks.

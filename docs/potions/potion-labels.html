Ingredient Shorthand

  • B = Bone Dust
  • A = Amaranth
  • G = Ghost Pepper
  • V = Vile Glomphidious
  • D = Dogwheat
  • R = Rock Salt

How to Fold

Print single-sided. Cut out each label along the solid border. Fold on the center line (the long edge) so the ingredient shorthand and the instructions face inward. Players open the fold and read it in landscape orientation: shorthand on top, instructions on the bottom.

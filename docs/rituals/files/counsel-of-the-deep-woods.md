# The Counsel of the Deep Woods

There are questions no priest can answer and no book contains — questions whose answers lie only with the old powers that dwell in the Deep Woods of Chicken's Rest. The elder things are not kind, and they are not patient, but they are ancient beyond reckoning, and they have watched this land since before its first stone was laid. When your need is great enough to risk their attention, you may call upon them for counsel through this rite.

Be warned: the powers give nothing freely. Come with an offering fit for each you would summon, come with a clear question, and come with the sacred draught prepared — or do not come at all. The woods remember those who waste their time.

This ritual is generic; it may be worked to petition any one of the powers, or several at once, so long as each is honored properly.

## Drawing The Path

First, trace upon the ground the symbol of the Deep Woods — the winding path, as it appears here:

   ![The symbol of the Deep Woods](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/85/Smiley.svg/1280px-Smiley.svg.png)

The path must be drawn large enough to walk, with room at its heart for the sacred draught.

## The Circles of Summoning

Along the path, draw **one small circle for each entity you wish to contact** — no more, no fewer. A rite that draws a circle for a power you do not intend to call is an insult, and insults are repaid.

## The Offerings

In each small circle, place **an offering suitable to the entity** whose circle it is. What suits one power offends another; know well who you are calling before you choose. A gift that pleases the intended power opens its ear; a careless or unfitting offering closes it — or worse, draws the wrong sort of notice.

## The Calling

Standing upon the path, **call out to each entity in turn by its name, and name aloud the offering you have laid for it.** Speak clearly. The powers do not lean in to listen to mumblers.

## The Question

When each has been called and each offering named, **ask your question** — plainly, and but once. Ask for counsel or advice; the woods answer questions, they do not grant wishes.

## The Sacred Draught

Finally, **place a potion brewed of Amaranth and Ghost Pepper in the center of the path.** This draught — the flower that will not fade married to the fire that will not die — is the price of an answer and the door through which it comes. Without it, the words spoken over the circles are only words, and the Deep Woods stay silent.

If the offerings were fitting and the draught true, an answer will come — in dream, in sign, or in a voice that is not quite a voice. Take what you are given. To demand more is to be given something you will wish you had not.

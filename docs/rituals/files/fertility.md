# A Blessing of Renewal

My daughter. I know you may not wish to hear from me, and I know you may set this letter aside unread. But if you have made it this far, then let me share with you a truth that every gardener knows — one which the scholars and the battlemages seem forever to forget: nothing in this world is truly spent. The leaf falls and feeds the soil. The soil feeds the seed. The seed becomes the tree, and in time its leaves fall, too. It is a circle older than any magic, and yet it *is* magic — the oldest kind there is.

I have spent my years learning to listen to that circle, to coax it along when it grows sluggish, and to thank it when it turns freely. What I offer you now is a small kindness I learned from the earth itself: a way to return what has been exhausted to a state of usefulness once more. It will not conjure something from nothing — that is not how the world works, no matter what anyone tells you — but it can breathe new life into what has been given up for spent.

I know this is not what you wanted from me. Use it or don't — that is yours to decide. But the earth gives freely, even to those who are angry with her. I hope you will forgive me for passing you this humble bit of magic, where other mothers would have passed their best pie recipes.

## Necessary Components

- Seeds (Item tags, not real seeds)

## Steps

1. **Sow the seeds.** Place them upon bare ground, gently, as you would if you meant for them to grow. Speak to them if you like — I always do. It does not matter what you say, only that you mean it kindly.

2. **Speak the blessing.** Stand over the seeds and say these words aloud:

   > *What was given, I return.*
   > *What was spent, I ask the earth to mend.*
   > *Root and rain and patient sun,*
   > *let what has ended begin again.*

3. **Wait, and be patient.** Sit with the seeds for ten minutes. Do not rush this. The earth moves at her own pace and does not care for hurrying. You may hum, or tend to other quiet work, but do not leave the seeds unattended.

## Effects

When the blessing has taken hold, your seeds sprout into plants.  (each seed you have sown may be exchanged: place it in the used components vessel, and take one used component back)

# The Illumination of Lies

Lies, untruths, forgery, libel — each of these is a tool of villains, crooks, cowards, and demons. With these tools in their arsenal, the enemies of the light drag us into darkness and cast doubt upon the words of the true. It is this doubt which is their most toxic poison: a seed of evil that plants itself in the hearts of the wronged and clouds their eyes. It isolates us. It draws lines between us, until we look upon even our closest siblings and friends and wonder if they, too, cast darkness upon us.

If I held the power to banish every lie from the face of this earth, I would do so in an instant. If I could burn a bit of sage and, by its smoke, cause all those who had ever deliberately misled their fellows to descend into a fit of hacking coughs which did not end until their insides spilled out of them and onto the ground, I would light a bonfire of that blessed plant.

But alas, I cannot. I can only perform this simple ritual, and so I write it down here that it may serve as an antidote to that doubt — a light which restores trust for those who have found themselves lost upon those dark plains of suspicion.

## Drawing The Circle

The first step is the hardest. This symbol must be drawn in the earth, exactly as it appears here:

   ![Soul symbol](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/85/Smiley.svg/1280px-Smiley.svg.png)

The symbol must be drawn large enough to contain the subject.

## The Elixir

The subject must take a dose of Silverwash. It is a property of Silverwash that it cannot be forced upon someone, so this trial may only be undertaken willingly. It cannot, unfortunately, be used to compel those of darkness to spill their secrets.

## Incantation

Finally, speak this incantation loudly, such that the subject can hear:

> *The elixir and this circle free you from doubt,*
> *for as long as the Silverwash affects you, you can speak no lie.*
> *The circle will not force you to speak, nor will it stop you from leaving.*
> *Do you understand?*

_Writings of the disgraced paladin Eamon of Daliah_

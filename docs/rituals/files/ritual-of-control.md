# The Ritual of Control

*From the archive of Trayvor of Thandle, set down in the winter of my years — that what was done to us shall not be forgotten, and that some soul, in some better age, may yet undo it.*

---

All rejoice, for Myrkaul the Bloodless has left the world. His final act of cruelty his own undoing. He came to our town, as he came to Padfoot, Greenvale, and poor dessicated Granville before. Those of us brave enough to stand against him stood, and the rest fled from his arrival. 

It turned out to be a foolish plan, for those who fled were destroyed by his magic, while we who stood were bound to serve his dark ritual. It is to my shame that I participated in aiding the sorcerer, but there was no choice. His ritual required the wills of some few people, and he could not complete his profane art without our help.

## The Circle

First he commanded us to dig a great symbol in our earth. We used hoes and shovels to carve it, and it took us most of a day to do so.

![The symbol of the circle](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/85/Smiley.svg/1280px-Smiley.svg.png)

Not one of us understood the sign we drew. I think none living understood it, save him. When the last line met the first and the circle was closed, the air went still, and the birds stopped.

## The Orb

Then Myrkaul took his place at the heart of the circle, and in his hands he held an orb of **starmetal** — the iron of a fallen star, black and pitted and far heavier than any stone its size, cold to look upon though the day was warm. Where he had come by such a thing, none of us dared to ask. I have thought on it every day since, and I am no nearer an answer.

## The Call and the Answer

He taught us then the incantation. It was something unreal, having the great sorcerer depend upon us. Growing frustrated as we struggled to learn the words. But any time his frustration grew too great, one of our number met a grisly fate, and all our efforts were reoubled. At last he was confident none would oppose him, and

**He called:** *"By star and by stone, by blood and by breath — who shall hold the magic of the world?"*

**We answered:** *"Thou alone. Thou alone, and none beside thee."*

Three times. No more, no fewer. I remember the third answer leaving me like a breath I could not draw back in — and I remember nothing after.

## What Followed

What followed, I cannot set down, for no one who stood in that circle remembers it. When we awoke, the sun had crossed half the sky. Myrkaul was gone, and the orb with him, and the circle was only lines in the dirt.

And magic was gone from the world.

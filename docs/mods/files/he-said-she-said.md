# He Said, She Said

**Timing:** This mod **opens the game** — it starts as soon as players are on the field.

**Characters:** 2

- **The Craftsperson** — a peasant artisan who made something on commission and was never paid.
  - **Starts with:** a copy of the truth-telling ritual (*The Illumination of Lies*), **1 Dogwheat**, and **1 Vile Glomphidious** — the two ingredients that brew **Moon Oil**, the draught whose effect is Silverwash. (Check the ritual for how they're used.)
  - Decide what you made: a pair of boots, a carved chest, a wedding gown. You are *right* — you did everything that was asked.
- **The Noble** — the patron who commissioned the work and refuses to pay.
  - **Starts with 3 coins.**
  - You are *wrong*, and you know it: the work was fine. You're refusing to pay because you can.

## The Story

The Craftsperson delivered the commission. The Noble claims it wasn't to specification and arrived late. The Craftsperson says they did all that was asked and wants the Noble **forced to buy it**. Nobody saw the original agreement. Nobody can prove anything.

## Plot

1. **Open the game mid-argument.** Walk into town already at each other: the Craftsperson demanding payment, the Noble waving it off. Draw a crowd — this is the first thing the players see.
2. **The Noble stonewalls.** Your line, again and again, with a shrug: *"Well, it's he said / she said. There's nothing we can do."* Be perfectly pleasant and perfectly immovable. Offer no evidence; demand theirs.
3. **Let the players try to fix it.** They will mediate, threaten, investigate, appeal to the Mayor or the Justice. Let them. The argument survives all of it — there is simply no way to know who is telling the truth.
4. **The Craftsperson produces the ritual.** After the argument has run long enough for "nothing we can do" to land a few times, the Craftsperson draws out the ritual page: *"Fine. There IS a way to know."* You know what it does and roughly how it's done — and you are carrying the makings of the draught.
5. **Do the ritual on the spot.** Draw the circle in the dirt, brew the Moon Oil (the two herbs you carry), and perform the ritual right there in front of everyone. The players should do the work; the Craftsperson directs.
6. **The Noble is surprised when it works.** This is the beat the whole mod hangs on. The Noble scoffs — peasant superstition — then agrees to the trial, *certain* it's nonsense... and then finds, to open horror, that the lies will not come out. **Drink willingly** (Silverwash cannot be forced on anyone; the Noble must choose to drink, and should — the trap is pride).
7. **The verdict.** Inside the circle, under Silverwash, the Noble cannot speak a lie. Let the crowd ask the questions. The Noble confesses — the work was to specification, it was on time, the plan was always to never pay.

## Resolution

- **The Noble pays.** 3 coins is everything they carry; the crowd will see it handed over, plus whatever apology the town finds funny.
- **The town learns the ritual exists.** This is the real payload of the mod — from minute one of the game, everyone has watched magic *work*. Let the Craftsperson be talked out of the ritual page, or sell it, or be robbed for it. It's loose in the world now.

## Special Considerations

- **The Noble must not confess early.** Everything before the ritual is *he said / she said*; the mod only works if the players feel the impasse before the ritual breaks it.
- **The Noble must be genuinely surprised.** Play the scoff, play the confidence, then play the dawning horror when the first "no" to an easy question simply... won't come out. The performance teaches the players the rule.
- **Keep it fast once the ritual starts.** Brewing and circle-drawing are the players' toy — point at the potion station and let them run.
- **If nobody helps**, escalate the argument on your own: the Craftsperson can begin scratching the circle with a stick while still shouting. Curiosity will do the rest.

## Props

- **Copy of *The Illumination of Lies*** — the truth-telling ritual page.
- **1 Dogwheat + 1 Vile Glomphidious** — the Craftsperson's herbs.
- **3 coins** — the Noble's purse.

# Necromancer Attack

**Characters:** 2

- **Necromancer** — a dark practitioner who has bound a ghost to their will.
  - **Item: The Chain** — a length of chain that binds the ghost to the necromancer. As long as they hold it, the ghost must obey.
- **Ghost** — a restless spirit dragged from its rest and enslaved. Must first read the [Ghost Player Instructions](../indymods/indymod.html?file=ghost-instructions).
  - Wears a **white headband** to mark them as a spirit.

## Setup

- The ghost reads the [Ghost Player Instructions](../indymods/indymod.html?file=ghost-instructions) and puts on a **white headband**.
- The necromancer holds one end of **The Chain**; the ghost holds (or is loosely tethered to) the other. This chain is the visible sign of the necromancer's control.

## Plot

The necromancer arrives in town leading their bound ghost on a chain, looking to menace, rob, or terrorize the townsfolk. The ghost does the necromancer's bidding — attacking whom they're told, fetching, threatening — but only because it is bound.

The necromancer commands; the ghost obeys. Point at a target, and the ghost advances (with the slow, obvious, controlled swings all ghosts use). The chain is the leash.

## Resolution

- **Break the necromancer's hold on the chain.** If the players can separate the necromancer from the chain — knocking them out, disarming them, tricking them into dropping it, cutting the chain, or otherwise breaking the connection — the ghost is freed and no longer has to obey.
- **Defeat the necromancer.** If the necromancer is knocked out or killed, the control ends and the ghost is released.
- **Free the ghost directly.** The usual ghost releases still work — a sacred bone, true love, or putting the spirit's soul at ease (see the ghost instructions). A freed ghost turns on the necromancer or simply drifts away to rest.

Remember: the ghost **cannot be harmed by weapons** (see the ghost rules). The real target is the necromancer and the chain that binds them together.

## Reward

Whatever the necromancer is carrying. Feel free to give them a few coins and an ingredient or two to make defeating them worthwhile.

## Special Considerations

- **Necromancer:** You are only as dangerous as your ghost. If you lose the chain or the players separate you from your ghost, play up your panic — you're far weaker without your servant. Be ready to flee or beg.
- **Ghost:** You obey the chain, not your own will. If freed mid-fight, you may turn on the necromancer or simply fade to rest — your call. Follow all the standard ghost rules.
- **The Chain** is a physical prop — handle it safely, keep it loose, and drop it instantly if anyone taps out.
- If the players find a clever way to break the necromancer's control, reward it.

## After the First Run

**Once this mod has been run once, it becomes a grab-bag mod.** Return the instructions, the white headband, and The Chain to the grab-bag binder. From then on it can be pulled and run ad-hoc like any other grab-bag encounter, rather than as a scheduled mod.

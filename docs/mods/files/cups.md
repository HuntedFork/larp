# Cups

**Characters:** 1 — the Rat Witch (see the *Rat Witch* mod).

The Rat Witch comes to town to play a game of **cups**. She sets up her cup row and invites the townsfolk to try their luck — but the cups hide her treasures on one side and her experiments on the other.

## What She Brings

Her treasures (won one at a time, on a win):

- **A Key**
- **The Iron God's artifact box** (see *Setup Reminder Sheet*)
- **Clue 7** — *The Goat-Kin's Record of the Madness*
- **A potion or two** (random)
- **A page of curses** — identical to the curse list for the book (print from the **Curses** section)

Her experiments (hidden in the losing cups):

- **Poison** *(new component)* — formatted like a potion; print the cards from the **Poison Cards (printable)** sheet (8 to a page). It reads:
  > *"You feel horribly nauseous. You are Injured for the next 10 minutes or until you vomit (not for real)."*
- **Various herbs**

## How the Game Works

1. She lines up the cups and sets them out. **One of the cups "wins"; the rest hold her experiments.**
2. Each round she **shuffles the cups** and then **pushes one cup forwards** toward the player.
3. **The trick (the Rat Witch knows it; the players may work it out): the winning cup is always the one to the _left_ of the cup she pushes forwards.** The cup she pushes is never the winner — it is a feint.
4. The player picks a cup and turns it over.

## Winning and Losing

- **On a win:** the player takes **one of her treasures**. She pays out from her stock — one prize per win.
- **On a loss:** the player has disturbed one of her experiments. They get whatever was in the cup — most often the **Poison** (they read it and suffer its effect) — and **they cannot play again until they have cleared their sickness** (wait out the 10 minutes / "vomit" per the Poison card, or otherwise cure the Injury).

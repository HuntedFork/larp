# Antidote Fight

**Timing:** Run at will — this mod is not scheduled (see the *Game Timeline*, Optional).

**Characters:** 2

- **The Merchant Peasant** — a commoner whose late father's business was seized out of spite.
  - **Starts with the antidote** (the small glass bottle).
- **The Noble Brat** — the young noble who took it, and the better blade of the two.

## The Story

The peasant's father ran a business, and he had a loan due. Any reasonable creditor would have written an extension. **The Noble Brat refused** — and took the business instead, purely out of spite and pettiness.

The Merchant Peasant challenged the Noble Brat to a **duel** over it. But the peasant knows perfectly well they are the worse swordsman, so they had a **poison and its antidote** made. The plan: poison them both, then win the fight that actually matters.

## Plot

1. **The duel.** The Merchant Peasant pulls a Hamlet — poisoning the blades — and the two duel over the seized business.
2. **The peasant loses the duel.** The Noble Brat disarms them and stabs them back with the poisoned blade. **Both are now poisoned.**
3. **The antidote drops.** The bottle ends up on the ground between them.
4. **Fisticuffs.** No more blades — the two brawl over the antidote in front of the whole town.
5. **Get detained.** Allow the townsfolk to pull you apart and hold you. You are both now at the town's mercy, poisoned, with one bottle between you.
6. **Make your case.** Each of you argues to the players why *you* should be the one to drink it. This is the heart of the mod — the players decide who lives.

## The Antidote Rules

- **After 10 minutes, anyone who has not drunk the entire antidote dies.** Start counting from the stabbing.
- **There is only one bottle**, and a full dose is the whole thing.
- **If you drink only half the antidote, flip a coin. On tails, you die.** Splitting it is a real option — it just isn't a safe one.

## Running Instructions

- **Full duel or straight to the brawl — your call.** If the town is busy and watching, play out the duel. If you want it quick, open at the stabbing.
- **Drop the antidote and go to full brawling.** The bottle must leave your hands and become a thing the town can see, grab, and argue over.
- **Allow yourselves to be detained.** Do not fight off the players. The moment someone intervenes, be restrained.
- **Then argue.** Plead, bargain, bribe, appeal to justice or to pity. Let the players hold the bottle and hold the decision.

## Resolution

However the players rule, it sticks. Common outcomes:

- **One of you drinks it all** and lives; the other dies when the 10 minutes are up.
- **The town splits it** — both of you drink half and both flip. Tails dies.
- **The players keep it** or dither past the deadline, and you both die in the street. This is a legitimate ending. Play it for all the horror it's worth.

## Reward

The players end up holding the antidote and the verdict. If they save the Noble Brat, they can press them hard for a reward — coins, or the return of the business to the peasant's family. If they save the Merchant Peasant, they have made a grateful friend with nothing much to give but their thanks.

## Props

- **Small glass bottle** — the antidote.

## Special Considerations

- **Standard combat safety applies:** slow, obvious, controlled swings; pull your blows. The fisticuffs are theatrical — **no real grappling and no real punches.** Sell it with noise and effort, not contact.
- **Break instantly if anyone taps out.**
- **Neither of you is purely in the right.** The Brat is petty and cruel; the Peasant poisoned two people over a debt. Give the players a genuinely hard call.
- **Do not resolve it yourselves.** Do not lunge for the bottle once detained, and do not concede early. The decision belongs to the town.
- **Keep the clock honest.** Ten minutes is short. Announce it clearly when the poison lands so the players know they are on a timer.

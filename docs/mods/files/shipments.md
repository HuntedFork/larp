# Shipments

**Characters:** 1 — the Courier, riding in from **Grand Harbor** with the merchants' goods.

There are **two merchants** in town, so every shipment is really **two shipments** — one box per merchant. Each round, bring both.

## First Shipment

**Costs 20 coins per merchant.** Each merchant's box includes:

- 3 each amaranth, dogwheat, rock salt
- 2 each ghost pepper, bone dust, vile glomphidious
- 1 Potion of Borrowed Dreams
- 1 Ritual of Binding / 1 Box of Fish God
- If the cursed book isn't in play yet, it's in here

**How it runs:** Load a box with the goods and bring it to town. Collect payment, hand over the goods, and **ask what the merchant would like fetched from Grand Harbor** — write their order down. That order is what you bring back on the Second Shipment.

## Second Shipment

**Come to town with the merchant's order**, whatever they asked you to fetch from Grand Harbor — plus **a pile of various herbs and any components that need to go out** (leftover stock, ritual pages, clues, anything else waiting to enter play).

The extra goods are for sale, priced as follows:

- 1 coin for amaranth / dogwheat / rock salt
- 2 coins for ghost pepper / bone dust / vile glomphidious
- **Clue 6** (*The Forge-Warden's Testament*) — 3 coins, along with any other clue that
  hasn't found an owner yet

## Special Considerations

- **Both merchants, every time.** The two merchants are rivals — do not let one see the other's order, and do not play favorites on price. (Unless a merchant bribes you. A small gratuity greases many wheels.)
- **The order is the fun of it.** Take the merchant's request seriously, no matter how strange. If they ask for something that doesn't exist, bring back the closest thing you can invent with a straight face.
- **Cash on delivery.** No coin, no box. If a merchant can't pay, hold their goods and let them scramble — the fee is due before the box opens.
- **You are a courier, not a fighter.** If waylaid, the goods can be stolen. That's a legitimate outcome; make the thieves work for it, then loudly report the crime to whoever passes for the law.

## Props

- **Two shipment boxes** — one per merchant, loaded per the lists above. These are the same boxes the Merchant Starter Packs came in (see the *Setup* doc) — collect them from the merchants after they've unpacked.
- **A ledger or scrap of paper** — to write down each merchant's Grand Harbor order.

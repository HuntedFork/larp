# The Rat Witch

**Characters:** 1 — the Rat Witch.

The Rat Witch comes to town to sell her goods. She brings an assortment of goods and is interested in purchasing additional goods.

**She will NOT accept money**, or values it much lower than any other currency.

## What She Brings

- Assorted herbs
- **Clue 1** — *A Bestiary of the Heart*
- The Ritual of Banishment
- A Pirate's Brew (this is mostly what she's here for)
- Any and all potions

She also runs a game of **cups** (see the *Cups* mod), playing for her treasures — a Key, the Iron God's artifact box, **Clue 7** (*The Goat-Kin's Record of the Madness*), a potion or two, and a page of curses.

## Leaving

She will attempt to steal one thing from the town when she leaves. She's pretty obvious about it, but puts it back if asked.

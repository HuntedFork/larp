# Falling Star

**Timing:** 4:15 (just before dusk — see the *Game Timeline* and the *Astrologer's* cryptic hint).

**Characters:** 1

- **The Researcher (Junior Truth Seeker Aldric Penn)** — the scholar who has been mailing the Librarian all day (see the *Pile o' Post*). He finally arrives in town in person, out of breath and full of dread. He carries the **final ritual (Asking the Gods)** with him.

## Setup

- The researcher should have already sent his four posts ahead of him (the **Pile o' Post**). By the time he arrives, the players should have received his warnings — the healing superstitions, the rituals hidden in family books, the ghost he saw, and his final desperate note.
- The researcher arrives carrying the **final ritual (Asking the Gods)**.
- Have the **starmetal** prop (the fallen star) ready and hidden. It "falls" on cue during his speech.
- This is timed to land just before dusk, dovetailing with the **Astrologer's** foretold celestial event.

## Plot

The researcher rushes into town bearing a dire message. He tells everyone:

- There is **too much magic** in the world now — far too much — and it is what is causing the **ghost spirits to walk the earth**.
- It is vitally important that the townsfolk complete **two rituals**: the one he sent ahead by post (the **Ritual of Binding**) and the **final ritual** he now carries (**Asking the Gods**).
- But there is a catch — the final ritual requires **starmetal**, and he despairs that there is no way to acquire any. Where would one even find such a thing?

At that very moment — **the star falls.** A streak of light, and the starmetal crashes to earth (reveal the prop). The impossible has just been handed to them. Now the players must move **quickly** to gather the starmetal and perform both rituals before it is too late.

## Resolution

- The players must **recover the fallen starmetal** and use it to complete the two rituals:
  1. The **Ritual of Binding** (mailed ahead in the Pile o' Post).
  2. The **final ritual — Asking the Gods** (carried in by the researcher).
- Completing both rituals is the climax of the day: it is the intended path to setting things right (binding the excess magic and calling on the gods).
- Play up the urgency — this happens near the end of the game, just before dusk, so keep the pressure on.

## Special Considerations

- **The Researcher:** You are exhausted, frightened, and earnest. You have traveled fast and hard (see your final post — you left immediately, but were slower than the messenger). You genuinely believe the world is in danger. Sell the despair about the starmetal so the star's fall lands as a moment of hope.
- **Timing the star:** The star should fall *right* as the researcher laments that starmetal is impossible to find. Coordinate the reveal for maximum effect.
- **Two rituals:** Make sure the Ritual of Binding (from the post) and the Asking the Gods ritual are both available and that players understand both are needed.
- Handle the **starmetal** prop safely.

# Terrible Healing (WIP)

**Characters:**

- **Professor Valinfar** (Forrest)
- **Melody**, Green Bend Chandler (Kenzie)
  - Melody has a soreness of the hands, which makes it difficult for her to make candles as much as she would like.
- **Jenny**, Melody's daughter

## Plot

Professor starts in town. He can provide healing, and is enamored with magic.

Jenny brings her mother Melody to town. Melody can sell some herbs to people, and is looking for medicine for her hands.

Professor Valinfar knows a ritual that can heal Melody's hand. He has it on him. _(TODO: the ritual)_

Melody is then possessed by the spirit of a demon, and attacks Professor Valinfar. Holding him up and laughing about her power.

Jenny begs the players not to kill her mom.

**If Melody is not killed:** The demon eventually kills Professor Valinfar. If the players use the ritual to unpossess someone then she comes back to herself. She remembers none of the experience.

**If Melody is killed:** Anyone who attacks the mother kills her immediately. Lie on the ground dead for an extended time to emphasise the horror. Professor Valinfar makes preparations to leave for the big city immediately. Jenny hangs around until someone agrees to take care of her. Valinfar will take her to a boarding school if prompted.

## Reward

It's a dubious reward, but the players keep Valinfar's ritual. If she survives, Melody will give them some herbs from her garden, otherwise they can be taken from her corpse. Valinfar can be easily pressed into giving some hush money.

# Dance Fighting (optional)

**Characters:** several — bandits (3–5 works well).

Several bandits come to town looking for easy money. This mod is **optional** — skip it if the day is already full.

## Plot

1. The bandits swagger into town and make their demand: **10 silver**, or they'll make *everyone* pay.
2. They posture, threaten, and give the town a short count to come up with the coin. Build the tension — this should feel like a fight is inevitable.
3. **Just as combat is about to break out, a silly tune begins to play.** (The storyteller starts the music the instant blades come out.)
4. The music compels everyone: **everyone has to dance while they fight — the bandits included.** Nobody knows where the tune comes from. The bandits are as confused as the players, but they dance anyway.

## The Dance-Fighting Rules

- While the tune plays, **anyone fighting must keep dancing** — footwork, spins, flourishes. Attacks land as normal, but they must be delivered *in dance*.
- **If you stop dancing, you're out of the fight.** Step back, catch your breath, and you may rejoin on the next verse if you start dancing again.
- Standard combat safety applies: slow, obvious, controlled swings; pull your blows. Players can only safely take **1 hit**; each bandit goes down after **2 hits**.
- When the song ends, the fight ends with it — whoever is still standing (dancing) holds the field. The bandits flee if they're clearly losing.

## Resolutions

- **Pay them off:** if the town hands over the 10 silver — before the fight, or mid-dance tossed into a hat — the bandits take it and leave, grumbling about the weird town.
- **Beat them dancing:** if the players best the bandits in the dance-fight, the bandits flee with nothing.
- Either way, the bandits never leave with more than the 10 silver.

## Special Considerations

- **Queue the silly tune ahead of time** so it can start the instant combat breaks out. Pick something unmistakably silly — the more undignified, the better.
- The bandits should commit to the bit: furious, menacing, and absolutely dancing.
- Let players use the rules creatively — a player who dances beautifully while never throwing a punch is playing it exactly right.

# Priest of the Old Gods

**Characters:** 1 — Ungulus, priest of the Old Ways.

**Before you begin:** Ungulus is a beastman — he must first read the [Beastman Player Instructions](../indymods/indymod.html?file=beastman-instructions).

A shabby beastman shuffles into town, bent under the weight of a heavy box. He is **Ungulus**, a practitioner of **The Old Ways**, and the box he carries is **the Bear God's artifact box** (see the *Setup Reminder Sheet*) — inside it are **Ursgrom's paw** and the Bear God page. He has been carrying this relic a long, long time. He is very, very hungry.

## What He Wants

- **Food. Above all else, food.** He is starving, and not picky — beastmen eat weird things. He will trade stories, blessings, and old lore for a meal.
- He will **not** trade the box. It is holy. He clutches it, naps on it, and mutters to it. But see below.

## How He Behaves

- **Erratically.** He is a beastman first and a priest second: he freezes mid-sentence, chews on things that are not food, screams at sudden noises, and bounces when excited.
- He preaches **The Old Ways** to anyone who will listen — rambling, half-coherent sermons about the old gods and how they sleep in the world.
- He is **100% peaceful** and flees from aggression, like any beastman. If driven off, he comes back. He is hungry.

## What He Knows (old god lore)

Share slowly, in fragments, mostly when fed:

- There are **three old gods**: **Ursgrom**, the Slumbering Mountain (the bear); **Uggglub**, the Drowned Choir (the river); and **Korrenvast**, the Turning Coin (the iron).
- The gods **sleep inside their relics**. The Old Ways teach that a mortal soul can **share space with a god** by opening the relic's box and **donning what lies within**. (He is, in effect, telling the players how the binding ritual works — let him say it in his own cracked words.)
- Of Ursgrom he speaks fondly: the Bear hungers for **honey and sweets**, is **stronger than anything**, believes **magic is too much work**, and would rather nap than reign.
- He does **not** know the mystery's answers. He is a holy fool, not a scholar — he knows prayers, not clues.

## The Box Gets Left Behind

**Somehow, the box ends up staying in town.** Do not make a show of it — lose it naturally in the bustle:

- He sets it down to eat and simply wanders off without it.
- If anyone tries to hand it back to him, he waves it away: the Old Ways say relics go where they will, and honestly the thing was getting heavy.
- Deep down he is **relieved to be rid of the burden** — he has carried it long enough, and if the town wants the Bear, the town can have the Bear.

Once the box is left behind, it is in play like any other artifact box (see the *Setup Reminder Sheet*).

## Leaving

Once he has eaten his fill and the box is "lost," Ungulus drifts back toward the Deep Woods, preaching to the trees as he goes.

## Props

- **Goat mask** — one, for Ungulus (see the *Props Checklist*).
- **Ursgrom (Bear) artifact box** — the paw + the Bear God page, stocked per the *Setup Reminder Sheet*. He carries it in; it stays in town.
- **Food** — whatever the town can feed him. He is not fussy.

## Special Considerations

- **He runs early, alongside the Rat Witch** (**12:30**), so that whoever binds themselves to Ursgrom gets most of the day as the Bear. Two NPCs are in town at once here — keep them apart so they don't compete for the same crowd.
- **The box must not leave with him.** However you play the scene, the mod has failed if Ungulus wanders off still holding the paw. If the players are too polite to keep it, have him forget it somewhere obvious and refuse it back.
- **He is a source of lore, not a puzzle.** Do not let him solve the mystery for anyone — he explains the *Old Ways* and how the gods are bound, nothing more.

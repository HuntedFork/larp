# The Three New Gods — Name Suggestions

*For the Bear God, the Fish God, and the Iron God. Each gets several options with
rationale. Pick one per god; strike the rest. These will be woven into the lore docs, so
they should sound at home beside **Nytheris**, **Thistlebarrow**, and **Ashroot**.*

Naming notes from last year's style:
- Returning gods have a **proper name** + an **epithet** ("Thistlebarrow, Lord of Horn
  and Branch"; "Nytheris, Keeper of the Glass Veil"; "Ashroot, the Rootbinder").
- Names tend to be earthy, slightly archaic, evocative of the creature's domain.
- So each suggestion below is given as **Name — "Epithet"**.

---

## The Bear God

Domain feel: old, mountainous, hibernation, guardianship, slow terrible strength,
seasons turning. Good candidate for **Earth** or **Ice** in the puzzle.

1. **Ursgrom — "the Slumbering Mountain"** *(recommended)*
   - "Urs-" reads unmistakably ursine; "-grom" lands like a low growl / a groaning peak.
   - Epithet plays on hibernation + his mass being mountain-like.
2. **Brammox — "Warden of the Long Sleep"**
   - Heavier, more brutal. Good if the Bear God is a fearsome guardian.
3. **Old Hollowden — "He Who Wakes in Hunger"**
   - Folk-name flavor (a den is where a bear sleeps). Reads like villagers named him.
4. **Cairngar — "the Stone-Furred"**
   - "Cairn" ties him to mountain stones/graves; pairs beautifully if Bear = Earth.

## The Fish God

Domain feel: deep water, rivers, tides, drowning, silence beneath the surface, the
River that "ran red with blood" in last year's chronicle. Good candidate for **Water**.

**LOCKED:** the Fish God is **Uggglub**. A guttural, gulping, unmistakably amphibian
name — reads like something the river itself burped up. Epithet options below; keeping
"the Drowned Choir" as the working epithet unless you prefer another.

1. **Uggglub — "the Drowned Choir"** *(locked name; working epithet)*
   - "Drowned Choir" nods to last year's river that "choked" and to song being silenced.
2. **Uggglub — "Coil of the Deep River"** *(alt epithet)*
3. **Uggglub — "the River's Hunger"** *(alt epithet)*
4. **Uggglub — "the Silver Shoal"** *(alt epithet)*


## The Iron God

Domain feel: forges, blood-iron, the one thing that **wounds Ashroot** (iron unsettles
her — a rich hook), war, ore, the unyielding. Good candidate for **Lightning/Storm**
(forge-spark) or **Fire** (the smithy). Deliberately NOT given Earth so as not to
collide with the Bear.

1. **Korrenvast — "the Turning Coin"** *(recommended, LOCKED epithet)*
   - Hard consonants, "vast" for a colossal ore-god; "the Turning Coin" = the iron veins
     scattered blind through the deep stone, and the great coin he flips to settle every
     wager. (Earlier draft epithet "the Unbending" was dropped when the Iron God was
     rewritten as a god of *fortune* rather than of discipline.)
2. **Hearthscar — "Lord of the Cold Forge"**
   - Evokes the smithy gone cold; good if the Iron God is fallen/abandoned.
3. **Grivmund — "the Blood-in-Ore"**
   - Ties iron to blood; resonates with the River of Blood and Ashroot's iron-weakness.
4. **Old Ferran — "the Nail That Holds the World"**
   - "Ferrum" = iron; folk-name flavor; humble-sounding but structurally cosmic.

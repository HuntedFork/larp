All 11 clues in one print job. Print DOUBLE-SIDED, flip on the SHORT edge. Each clue is exactly two pages, so every sheet comes out as one complete clue: cover on the front, lore on the back. You get 11 sheets, in clue order.
Then fold each sheet in half across the short edge (horizontal crease at the middle) with the cover facing out, and staple the open edge. Each becomes an 8.5 × 5.5 folded card.
Two to do by hand afterwards: tear Clue 8 in half (a half to each beastman), and put Clue 4 in the saint's box unopened.

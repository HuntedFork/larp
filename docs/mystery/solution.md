## What "Solving It" Means at the Table

A group has solved the mystery when they can correctly state, for **all six** gods, their
**ingredient** (and therefore their element). Because element is locked to ingredient,
naming either one names both. Partial credit / staged reveals are up to the Storyteller.
The single most important hidden truth the grid reveals:

> **Nytheris** — the newcomer the villagers do not recognize as a god at all — is the one
> being tied to **Spirit / Amaranth**: the reagent of the soul and self. She is the hidden
> **god of madness**, the hollowing of the spirit, and pinning her down is the climax of
> the mystery.

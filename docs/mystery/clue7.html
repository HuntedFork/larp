Print DOUBLE-SIDED on one letter sheet (flip on the SHORT edge). Page 1 is the DO NOT OPEN cover; page 2 is the lore. Then fold the sheet in half across the short edge (horizontal crease at the middle) with the cover facing out. You get a 8.5 × 5.5 folded card: cover on the front, lore hidden inside. Staple the open edge.

Page 2 — Inside lore (prints on the back side)

Scratched on hide by one of the goat-kin who was made mad in the year of the hollowing and after was made well. The counting-marks are left as written, in fives.

The Record of the Madness — I make it so it is not forgot

I write with the hand though the hand is not the goat-way. The goat-way is the nose. Words lie; smell does not lie; still water lies most of all, for it makes a second face that is not you and I will not look in it. But I make these marks so the kids and the kids' kids will know the smell of the bad time before it comes again.

The bad time was Nytheris. Hear it and remember it: Nytheris is the one bound to the Spirit — she is the soul-thief, and it is the spirit, the inside-self, the part that is not meat, that she reaches in and takes. She came and she hollowed us. Five of my herd and five again she emptied, and their bodies walked but the inside was gone out of them like breath off a cold hill. It was not the meat she wanted. It was the soul. That is her whole nature and her only hunger.

(the marks here, in fives:) ||||| ||||| — ten I counted go empty before the mask came.

We were made well when the Ancient Mask was raised and drove her influence from the land, and the inside-selves came back into the bodies, and I could smell my own herd as my own herd again. So I set down the prayer of guarding, that we say against her return:

Great Bear, keep us —
for of the two beasts of blood, the Bear and the stag, it is the Bear who is the god of the Body, of the flesh and the sinew and the sleeping frame that the soul-thief cannot hollow;
the stag is a beast of blood also, but the stag is not the Body's god and never was — his power lies otherwise, and it is not to the Bear's keeping we should look for it.
Keep our meat whole, Great Bear, that our souls have a house to come home to.

Smell this and know it. The soul is Nytheris's country. The Body is the Bear's. Do not confuse the stag for either.

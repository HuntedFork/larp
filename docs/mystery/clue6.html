Print DOUBLE-SIDED on one letter sheet (flip on the SHORT edge). Page 1 is the cover; page 2 is the lore. Then fold the sheet in half across the short edge (horizontal crease at the middle) with the cover facing out. You get a 8.5 × 5.5 folded card: cover on the front, lore hidden inside. Staple the open edge.

Page 2 — Inside lore (prints on the back side)

The Testament of Dorgan Ash, Forge-Warden of the deep mine at Chicken's Rest — set down for whoever takes up the hammer after him.

To the one who takes the forge after me

I have worked the deep seam forty years, and I will tell you the one thing that matters before I tell you any other: know whose ground you dig in, and learn how he plays. The smiths who forget it are the smiths we bury in the collapse.

Every stroke of the pick is a wager laid before Korrenvast, whom the old folk name the Iron God. And mark this well — the Iron God holds the whole of the Earth for his own; the deep stone, the buried ore, the roots of the mountain and every vein of metal in it are his dominion and no one else's. Ore and stone are kin, the miners say, and both belong to him. But he does not lay his metal out in tidy rows for the diligent. He scatters it blind — a seam here, nothing for a hundred fathoms, then a king's ransom behind a wall no sensible warden would ever have struck. No survey has ever promised a vein and been believed twice.

(scratched deeper into the page:) Call it before you swing. Heads or tails, and no whining after. He has no patience for a man who will not name his side.

So do not dig where it is safe. The rich seam has never once gone to the careful; it goes to the bold hand and the lucky one, and the Iron God favours no other kind. Wager well, pay out when you lose, and the deep may keep you. Hedge and dither and you will learn — as better wardens than you have learned — that the Earth belongs to the Iron God, and he settles every bet.

— Dorgan Ash, Forge-Warden

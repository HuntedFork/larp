Print DOUBLE-SIDED on one letter sheet (flip on the SHORT edge). Page 1 is the DO NOT OPEN cover; page 2 is the lore. Then fold the sheet in half across the short edge (horizontal crease at the middle) with the cover facing out. You get a 8.5 × 5.5 folded card: cover on the front, lore hidden inside. Staple the open edge.

The Testament of Dorgan Ash, Forge-Warden of the deep mine at Chicken's Rest — set down for whoever takes up the hammer after him.

To the one who takes the forge after me

I have worked the deep seam forty years, and I will tell you the one thing that matters before I tell you any other: know whose ground you dig in, and give him his due. The smiths who forget it are the smiths we bury in the collapse.

Every stroke of the pick is done on the sufferance of Korrenvast, whom the old folk name the Iron God. And mark this well — the Iron God holds the whole of the Earth for his own; the deep stone, the buried ore, the roots of the mountain and every vein of metal in it are his dominion and no one else's. Ore and stone are kin, the miners say, and both belong to him. When the seam runs rich we thank Korrenvast; when the roof groans we beg his pardon. He is a slow god and a patient one, but the earth is his, root and rock and metal alike, and he does not forget a debt.

(scratched deeper into the page:) Leave the first nugget of every new seam in the rock. It is his. Take it and the mountain takes you.

Do this and the deep will keep you. Forget it, and you will learn — as better wardens than you have learned — that the Earth belongs to the Iron God, and he collects.

— Dorgan Ash, Forge-Warden

Page 2 — Inside lore (prints on the back side)

A primer from the Library of Chicken's Rest, shelved under Local Powers & Elder Things: Of the Six Powers of the Deep Woods, and the Elements to Which They Are Bound.

The teaching of the six powers

Every child of Chicken's Rest is taught the same first lesson about the Deep Woods: that six elder powers dwell within them, no more and no fewer, and that each one of the six is bound to a single element — one power to one element, never two to the same. What no primer will tell you is which power holds which element, for on that point the scholars have quarreled for a century and the Library keeps no settled answer. That much you must learn elsewhere. Here we give only the names.

The six powers

The powers of the Deep Woods are these six, and only these:

Cindralok, the serpent of soot and ember;
Uggglub, the Fish God of the crossing;
Korrenvast, the Iron God of the deep mine;
Thistlebarrow, the great stag, Lord of Horn and Branch;
Nytheris, the Pale Reflection, She-Who-Covets;
and Ursgrom, the Bear, god of the steadfast frame.

The six elements

And the elements to which the powers are bound are the six known to every scholar — Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Spirit, and Body — one apportioned to each power, so that the six powers and the six elements answer to one another exactly, with none left over and none doubled.

(a reader's note in pencil:) Six powers, six elements, one each. So which is which? The Library won't say — but someone in this town must know.

Here ends the primer. For the particular bindings, seek the other records.

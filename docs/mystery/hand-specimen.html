Choosing the hand

Part 1 sets the whole document in a hand — that is what is now live in the clues. Part 2 compares hands for the margin notes only. Everything is real clue text at the size and column width it gets on the printed page, so this is what comes out of the printer.

Sizes are tuned per font so the samples share a physical x-height — the fair comparison, since a hand at a nominal 11pt prints much larger than a serif at 11pt.

This page is a working document for picking fonts. It is not a prop and is not on the print checklist — say the word and I'll delete it.

Body — Kalam · 10.5pt — currently live in the clues

Wailing Stew (serves six, or curses one)

Folk turn up their noses at my cooking until they've had a bowl of this, and then they don't turn up anything ever again. It is not a kind supper. But a recipe is a recipe, and mine are the best in three valleys, so mind the steps and don't you dare substitute.

Begin with a whole Ghost Pepper — and it must be the Ghost, for it is a thing of pure Fire and nothing else carries the flame into the pot. Char it black over the coals till the whole kitchen weeps, then drop it in.

Part 2 — the margin notes

The margin notes are by a different person than whoever wrote the document (“a different, shakier hand below”), so they use a second hand rather than the same one shrunk. Architects Daughter is live — it has no bold, which rules it out for body text but does not matter for a margin note.

1 — Kalam · 9.5pt · x-height 0.53 — same hand as the body; the margin stops reading as someone else

…and under it they unburdened; my right foot carried me down every dark road.

(in the same hand, smaller:) They keep my right hand and my right foot as relics still, wrapped in cloth. Rest them upon the troubled; the gift has not wholly left them.

(scrawled sideways:) Gloves, you fool. I lost a fingernail to this stew and I'd not lose you one too. — G.P.

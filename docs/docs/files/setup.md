# Setup Reminder Sheet

A quick reference for keeping track of merchant packs, shipments, and pricing.

## Merchant Starter Pack
- Library Setup

## Merchant Starter Pack

A **Merchant Starter Pack** includes:

- 5 dogwheat
- 4 each amaranth, rock salt
- 2 each of ghost pepper, bone dust
- 1 Moon Oil / Salve of Wound Closure recipe
- 1 Clue (each)

## First Shipment

The first shipment costs **20 coins** and includes:

- 3 each amaranth, dogwheat, rock salt
- 2 each ghost pepper, bone dust, vile glomphidious
- 1 Potion of Borrowed Dreams
- 1 Ritual of Binding / 1 Box of Fish God
- If the cursed book isn't in play yet, it's in here

## Third Shipment

The third shipment includes:

- All ingredients for sale, priced as follows:
  - 1 coin for amaranth / dogwheat / rock salt
  - 2 coins for ghost pepper / bone dust / vile glomphidious
  - Any leftover clues - 3 a piece

## Artifact Boxes (Gods)

Each god has an artifact box the binding player opens. Include the correct god page (see the **Gods** doc) and artifact item in each:

- **Ursgrom (Bear God):** the artifact item + the Bear God page.
- **Uggglub (River God):** the artifact item + **1 Vile Glomphidious** + the River God page.
- **Korrenvast (Iron God):** the artifact item — a **great iron coin** (his god icon, used for all his wagers) — + **5 coins** + the Iron God page.

**Note:** Korrenvast's power, *Fortune's Favor*, draws **10 coins from the coin box every hour**, which he may only stake on coin-flip bets. Keep the coin box well stocked.

## Pile o' Post

The Postperson starts with a **pile o' post** — four folded letters from Junior Truth Seeker **Aldric Penn**, each addressed to *The Librarian* and marked *DO NOT OPEN UNTIL* a time (12:00, 1:00, 2:00, 3:00). Print them from the **Pile o' Post** prop (see the *Docs* section). The pile, in order, contains:

- Post message 1
- Clue
- Post message 2
- Ritual of Binding
- Post message 3
- Asking the Gods Ritual
- Post message 4

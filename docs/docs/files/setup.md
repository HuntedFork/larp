# Setup Reminder Sheet

A quick reference for keeping track of merchant packs, shipments, and pricing.

## The Saint's Box (set up before game start)

Set up the saint's box before the game begins and place it in the play area. The box is
the saint's crypt — the *Record of the Consecration* (Clue 11) tells the players she was
buried in Chicken's Rest beneath the red tablecloth, and leads them to it.

The box needs:

- **Bones** — the saint's skeleton, in pieces. Assembling the bones satisfies the gate on
  Clue 4.
- **2× Bone Dust**
- **Clue 4** — *The Bones of the Saint* (its cover reads DO NOT OPEN until the bones are
  assembled)
- **1× Hand of the Saint card** (the right hand)
- **1× Foot of the Saint card** (the right foot)
- **Red tablecloth on top**

Print the Hand and Foot cards from the *Saint Relic Cards* prop (see the *Mystery*
section).

## Library Setup (set up before game start)

Shelve the two **premise documents** in the play area, where the Librarian and any Truth
Seeker can find them. Their covers read **PROPERTY OF THE LIBRARY**:

- **Clue 9** — *Of the Six Powers of the Deep Woods*
- **Clue 10** — *Of the Six Reagents of the Art*

These two name the six powers, the six reagents, and the six elements, but pair nothing.
They are what makes the rest of the clues legible — get them into play early.

## Merchant Starter Pack

A **Merchant Starter Pack** includes:

- 5 dogwheat
- 4 each amaranth, rock salt
- 2 each of ghost pepper, bone dust
- 1 Moon Oil / Salve of Wound Closure recipe
- 1 Clue (each) — **Clue 2** (*Gramma Pell's Receipt*) in one pack, **Clue 3** (*A Ruling of
  the Magistrate*) in the other. These two are load-bearing; the merchant packs are the only
  source guaranteed to be in town from the first minute.

**Pack each starter pack in its own box** — one box per merchant. The courier reuses these same two boxes for the First and Second Shipments.

## Shipments

Shipment contents and pricing now live in the *Shipments* mod (see the *Mods* section). Use the **two merchant starter pack boxes** as the shipment boxes, packed per the lists there.

## Artifact Boxes (Gods)

Each god has an artifact box the binding player opens. Include the correct god page (see the **Gods** doc) and artifact item in each:

- **Ursgrom (Bear God):** the artifact item (his paw) + the Bear God page. The box enters play with **Ungulus, the Priest of the Old Gods** (see the *Priest of the Old Gods* mod), who carries it into town and leaves it behind.
- **Uggglub (River God):** the artifact item + **1 Vile Glomphidious** + the River God page.
- **Korrenvast (Iron God):** the artifact item — a **great iron coin** (his god icon, used for all his wagers) — + **5 coins** + the Iron God page.

**Note:** Korrenvast's power, *Fortune's Favor*, draws **10 coins from the coin box every hour**, which he may only stake on coin-flip bets. Keep the coin box well stocked.

## Pile o' Post

The Postperson starts with a **pile o' post** — four folded letters from Junior Truth Seeker **Aldric Penn**, each addressed to *The Librarian* and marked *DO NOT OPEN UNTIL* a time (12:00, 1:00, 2:00, 3:00). Print them from the **Pile o' Post** prop (see the *Docs* section). The pile, in order, contains:

- Post message 1
- **Clue 11** — *The Record of the Consecration of the Saint* (the document Aldric returns
  to the library; it points the town at the saint's box)
- Post message 2
- Ritual of Binding
- Post message 3
- Asking the Gods Ritual
- Post message 4

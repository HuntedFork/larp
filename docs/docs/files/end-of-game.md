## When the Ritual Takes Hold

*(Storyteller: read aloud once the ritual succeeds.)*

> As the ritual takes hold, all of you find yourselves suddenly in a flowing, pure white, liminal space. As you orient yourself, you become aware of each other. Near you, all around you. Your minds are joined, and while you can't see each other you can sense each other. You find it would be easy to get lost in each other's consciousness, but the magic of the ritual takes hold and you feel your attention directed.
>
> You feel the sensation of flying, and are suddenly high above the world. A swirling purple vortex of energy is erupting out of the world... directly over Chicken's Rest.
>
> The magic of the ritual gives you great power to alter the world. Power to make a change. Power to control magic, if only for a few brief moments. What will you do with it?
>
> Your joined minds can commune with each other. You do not have forever, but you have long enough. Your power is not limitless, but it is vast. What will you do with it?
>
> If any have an idea what should be done while you hold the power, raise your hand and put forth your plan.

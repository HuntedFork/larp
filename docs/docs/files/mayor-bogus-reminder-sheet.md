# Mayor Bogus Reminder Sheet

A quick reference for keeping track of merchant packs, shipments, and pricing.

## Merchant Starter Pack

A **Merchant Starter Pack** includes:

- 2 each amaranth, dogwheat, rock salt
- 1 each of ghost pepper, bone dust
- 1 Moon Oil / Salve of Wound Closure recipe
- 2 Clue tokens

## First Shipment

The first shipment costs **20 coins** and includes:

- 3 each amaranth, dogwheat, rock salt
- 2 each ghost pepper, bone dust, vile glomphidious
- 1 Potion of Borrowed Dreams
- 1 Clue/Ritual
- If the cursed book isn't in play yet, it's in here

## Third Shipment

The third shipment includes:

- All ingredients for sale, priced as follows:
  - 1 coin for amaranth / dogwheat / rock salt
  - 2 coins for ghost pepper / bone dust / vile glomphidious

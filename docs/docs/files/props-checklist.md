# Props Checklist

*Master list of every physical prop needed to run the game, and where each one comes from.
Whenever a mod, character, doc, or ritual specifies a physical prop, add it here.*

*Paper props are listed here for placement only — for what to actually put through the
printer, see the **Print Checklist**.*

---

## Coins (silver pieces)

Coins are the currency of the whole day. Stock the coin box generously — it is drained
constantly and refilled rarely.

| Purse | Coins | Where it exists |
|-------|-------|-----------------|
| Player starting coins | **48** total | *Characters* — Justice 2, Healer 1, Scion 8 (×2), Constable 2, Scholar 2, Peasant 1 (×5), Bard 2, Notary 2, Town Crier 2, Astrologer 1, Postperson 2, Illiterate 1, Pirate Captain 2, Traveler 2 (×4) |
| The Noble's prize purse | **20** | *The Play* — paid out for the best performance |
| The Noble's purse | **3** | *He Said, She Said* — everything he carries; handed over at the verdict |
| The Visiting Merchant's budget | **6** | *Map and Portrait* |
| The Travelling Merchant | **6** | *Bridge Guardian* — haggled away before departing |
| The hunting Noble | **6** | *Deer In The Woods* — pays 1 per hunter |
| Professor Flombower | **10** | *Assassination* — loot on the body |
| The Villain | **5** | *Ghost Knight Fights* — robbed from others |
| Korrenvast's artifact box | **5** | *Setup* — packed in the Iron God box |
| **The coin box / money vessel** | **stock heavily** | Korrenvast draws **10 per hour** (*Fortune's Favor*); each Peasant draws **2 per hour** (*Peasant Work*); the Town Crier, Astrologer, Justice, and Scoundrel all draw from or return to it |

- [ ] **Coins** — counted out into the purses above, with the coin box stocked on top.
- [ ] **The great iron coin** — Korrenvast's god icon; a large, clearly two-sided coin he
      flips to settle every wager (*Fortune's Favor*). Big enough to flip and read at a
      distance. *Where: the Korrenvast artifact box (Setup).*

| **Amaranth** | 14 | Merchant Starter Packs (4 ea), First Shipment (3 ea) |
| **Rock Salt** | 16 | Merchant Starter Packs (4 ea), First Shipment (3 ea), Mad Alchemist 1, Herbalist 1 |

- [ ] **Used components vessel** — spent tags go in; the *Fertility* ritual trades a sown

## Costume & Marker Props (monster station / costume area)

- [ ] **White headbands** — mark a player as a ghost or spirit. *Where: one per ghost in
      Ghost Invasion (5), the bound ghost in Necromancer Attack (1), the Ghost Knight in
      Ghost Knight Fights (1), the beastman ghost in Beastman's Body (1), a spare for
      anyone summoned by the Summon Ghost Knight ritual, and **2–3 spares for Make Your Own
      Ghost** (run ad-hoc, any number of times, by whoever is free). Lives in the grab-bag
      binder.*
- [ ] **Blue wristbands** — Pro-Magic side. *Where: Professor Voss (Arguing Professors),
      plus spares at the monster station for players who pick a side.*
- [ ] **Green wristbands** — Anti-Magic side. *Where: Professor Grimsby (Arguing
      Professors), plus spares at the monster station.*
- [ ] **Goat masks** — mark a beastman. *Where: 2 for Beastmen Come to Town, 1 for Ungulus
      (Priest of the Old Gods), 1 for the travelling merchant (Bridge Guardian), and 2 for
      the body and the ghost in Beastman's Body — that ghost wears the mask **and** a white
      headband.*
- [ ] **Deer antlers** — *Where: the deer in Deer In The Woods; from the costume area.*
- [ ] **Fine clothes** — *Where: the Noble in The Play and the Visiting Merchant in Map and
      Portrait; both must look visibly richer than everyone else.*
- [ ] **Armour + a weapon with reach** — *Where: the Bridge Guardian; a heavily armoured
      giant whose eyes cannot be seen beneath a black helmet.*
- [ ] **Weapons (boffer)** — *Where: the Ghost Knight must have one; the Summon Ghost
      Knight ritual requires the caster to lay a weapon on the ground as the offering; plus
      general combat in Dance Fighting, Bridge Guardian, Antidote Fight, and the
      Highwayman's Rob power.*

## Mod-Specific Props

- [ ] **The Ancient Mask** — the mask that drove Nytheris from the land. *Where: kept by
      the storytellers. Beastmen are TERRIFIED of it (Beastman Instructions); raising it at
      the ghost in Beastman's Body ends that mod early.*
- [ ] **Chain** — the leash binding the ghost to the necromancer. *Where: Necromancer
      Attack. Handle safely, keep it loose, drop it instantly on a tap-out. Returns to the
      grab-bag binder after the first run.*
- [ ] **Cups** — a set for the shell game; one winning cup, the rest hold experiments.
      *Where: the Rat Witch (Cups).*
- [ ] **A Key** — *Where: one of the Rat Witch's cup-game treasures (Cups, The Rat Witch).*
- [ ] **Starmetal** (fallen-star prop) — black, pitted, heavy-looking. *Where: hidden and
      ready to "crash to earth" on cue in Falling Star; required by the final ritual, and
      named as the orb in The Ritual of Control.*
- [ ] **Music player + a silly tune** — queued before the mod starts. *Where: Dance
      Fighting; played the instant blades come out.*
- [ ] **Small glass bottle** — the antidote. *Where: the Merchant Peasant starts with it in
      Antidote Fight; it must end up on the ground between the duellists.*
- [ ] **Alcohol (or a stand-in)** — *Where: the tavern / Pirate Bar; what the beastmen trade
      their torn clue halves for (Beastmen Come to Town).*
- [ ] **Food** — *Where: whatever the town can feed Ungulus (Priest of the Old Gods). He is
      starving and not fussy.*
- [ ] **Paper + pens/pencils** — *Where: Map and Portrait (the commissioned map and portrait
      must actually get drawn), the Wanted Poster (culprit drawn and sentence written by
      hand), and the courier's ledger for the Grand Harbor orders (Shipments).*
- [ ] **Magic wedding ring** *(optional, 3D-printed)* — *Where: Ghost Invasion; slipping it
      on the Lover's finger with vows is an instant, guaranteed release.*

## Boxes

- [ ] **2 merchant boxes** — *Where: one per merchant. The Merchant Starter Packs are packed
      in these at setup, and the courier collects and reuses the same two boxes for the
      First and Second Shipments (Shipments, Setup).*
- [ ] **Ursgrom (Bear) artifact box** — the paw + the Bear God page. *Where: carried into
      town by Ungulus (Priest of the Old Gods) and left behind.*
- [ ] **Uggglub (River) artifact box** — the artifact item + **1 Vile Glomphidious** + the

      God page. *Where: one of the Rat Witch's cup-game treasures (Cups).*
- [ ] **The saint's box** (her crypt) — the bones in pieces, **2× Bone Dust**, **Clue 4**,

      (Clue 11) points players to it.*
- [ ] **Bones** — a skeleton in pieces. *Where: the saint's box (assembling them is the gate
      on Clue 4). Also the general ghost-dispelling prop — a sacred bone dissipates any
      ghost (Ghost Instructions).*

## Paper Props (placement)

*Print these from the **Print Checklist**; listed here so nothing goes missing from a box or
a purse.*

- [ ] **Character sheets** — one per player, with tear-off items intact.
- [ ] **Potion cards + potion labels** — *Where: the potion station, one slot per potion
      type; slot 12 is Pirate's Brew.*
- [ ] **Mishap cards** — *Where: the potion station.*
- [ ] **Curse cards** (9 to a page) — *Where: inside the Cursed Tome; a second identical page
      is one of the Rat Witch's cup-game treasures.*
- [ ] **Ghost curse cards** — 3 stacks of 8. *Where: the Counter, the Madman, and the Judge
      (Ghost Invasion).*
- [ ] **Poison cards** (8 to a page) — *"You feel horribly nauseous. You are Injured for the
      next 10 minutes or until you vomit (not for real)."* *Where: hidden under the losing
      cups (Cups).*
- [ ] **Snake oils** — 10 fold-over labels, 6 to a page (2 duds, 6 cure-alls, 1 "you feel
      amazing," and 1 bad one marked with a star on the outside so it can be dealt to an
      unlucky target on purpose).
- [ ] **God Prophecies** — six folded cards, one per god (Nytheris, Ursgrom, Uggglub,
      Korrenvast, Cindralok, Thistlebarrow), each giving that god's counsel on what to do
      about the magic. 4 to a page, double-sided, cut and fold.
- [ ] **God pages** — Bear, River, and Iron. *Where: one in each artifact box.*
- [ ] **Pile o' Post** — four folded letters to The Librarian, marked DO NOT OPEN UNTIL
      12:00 / 1:00 / 2:00 / 3:00, with **Clue 11** tucked in post 1 (the document Aldric
      returns to the library), the **Ritual of Binding** in post 2, and the **Asking the
      Gods** ritual in post 4. *Where: the Postperson (Setup).*
- [ ] **Wanted Poster** — folded double-sided with the *To: The Justice* cover out. Draw the
      culprit and write the *For* / *Punishment* by hand during the game. Bring pen(s).
- [ ] **Clues 1–11** — every clue has one assigned home. Pack them by the table below;
      nothing goes into the game unplaced.

| Clue | Document | Goes to | Enters play |
|------|----------|---------|-------------|
| **1** | *A Bestiary of the Heart* | The **Rat Witch's** stock — barter only, she won't take coin | 1:00 (*Rat Witch*) |
| **2** | *Gramma Pell's Receipt for Wailing Stew* | **Merchant Starter Pack A** — box it with the herbs | Game start (*Setup*) |
| **3** | *A Ruling of the Magistrate* | **Merchant Starter Pack B** — box it with the herbs | Game start (*Setup*) |
| **4** | *The Bones of the Saint* | **The saint's box**, under the red tablecloth — cover reads DO NOT OPEN until the bones are assembled | Set up before game start (*Setup*) |
| **5** | *A Letter on the River-God* | **The Scholar** — this is their *mysterious message*; hand it over with their sheet | Game start (*Characters*) |
| **6** | *The Forge-Warden's Testament* | **The Courier**, sold on the Second Shipment at 3 coins | 3:30 (*Shipments*) |
| **7** | *The Goat-Kin's Record of the Madness* | **Cups** — one of the Rat Witch's cup-game treasures | 2:30 (*Cups*) |
| **8** | *The Stag's Own Verse* | **The two beastmen — tear this one in half**, a half each; traded for alcohol | 2:00 (*Beastmen Come to Town*) |
| **9** | *Of the Six Powers of the Deep Woods* | **Library setup** — shelved in the play area, cover reads PROPERTY OF THE LIBRARY | Set up before game start (*Setup*) |
| **10** | *Of the Six Reagents of the Art* | **Library setup** — shelved in the play area, cover reads PROPERTY OF THE LIBRARY | Set up before game start (*Setup*) |
| **11** | *The Record of the Consecration of the Saint* | **Pile o' Post, post 1** — the document Aldric Penn returns to the library; points to the saint's box | 12:00 (*Setup*) |

*Placement notes: Clues **2, 3, and 4** are the load-bearing element→reagent links — 2 and 3
sit in the merchant packs because those are the only guaranteed, in-town-at-noon sources.
Clue **8** is the one document the puzzle can survive losing (Air/Dogwheat falls out by
elimination), which is why it is the one that gets torn in half and drunk away. Clue **11**
lands at noon so the saint's box is found early enough to matter.*
- [ ] **Saint relic cards** — 1× Hand of the Saint, 1× Foot of the Saint. *Where: the saint's
      box.*
- [ ] **Solution Key** — one copy, kept hidden. *Where: the storytellers.*

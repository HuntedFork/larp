# The Cursed Tome

*Storyteller / prop document for the Illiterate character and their Cursed Tome.*

---

## Printing & Folding Instructions

- **The Tome:** Print the "The Tome" section below and fold it in half.
  - The **outside** should read: *"Only opened by someone who can read."*
  - The **inside** contains Cindralok's message.
- **The Purified Page:** Print the "When All Curses Have Been Taken" section and fold it in half.
  - The **outside** should read: *"Open when all curses have been taken from the book."*
  - The **inside** contains the tome's final blank-page reveal.
- The **List of Curses** is a separate print (9 to a page). See the **Curses** tab.

> **Note for Storytellers:** When someone takes a curse from this book, they can pick and choose the curse. No one is stuck with a curse they do not want. Curses last until game end, or until it's not fun for the player anymore. Players in for a challenge should pick one at random.

---

## The Tome

### Outside (front of the fold)

> **Only opened by someone who can read**

### Inside

This tome is clearly cursed. You've never seen a more cursed object in your life. The curses are radiating off it in an almost purple glow. Reading any further will curse you. Take a curse from the book before continuing (or slam the book shut to avoid taking one).

---

*"Cindralok's Vengeance.*

*I am Cindralok. Ancient Fire Serpent of the deep woods. 5 years ago, this book was stolen from me. I have erased the knowledge contained within and replaced it with my ancient hatred. 5 years from now, if it has not been returned to me, my vengeance shall take form, and the book, its owner, their family, and every building they have ever set foot in will catch fire and they will burn.*

*Dated: `<a date that is exactly 5 years ago today>`"*

---

As curses are taken from the book, you sense the dread energy emanating from it disperses. You sense you can prevent the terrible destiny by taking each curse from the book.

---

## When All Curses Have Been Taken

### Outside (front of the fold)

> **Open when all curses have been taken from the book**

### Inside

The last of the energy finally fades from the book. Its curse is cleared and it is purified. Inside has become blank except for a single page which reads:

> *"What wouldst thou ask?"*

You can write a single question here and bring it to a Storyteller for a single answer.

# The Cursed Tome

*Storyteller doc for the Illiterate character and their Cursed Tome. The prop itself is the
printable — **The Cursed Tome (printable)** in the Docs index (`cursed-tome.html`). Print and
fold that; this page is the storyteller's how-to-run.*

---

## What the prop is

A book (any old hardback will do) with three things tucked into it:

1. **The tome leaf** — folded, cover out: *"Only opened by someone who can read."* Inside is
   Cindralok's message. This sits at the front of the book.
2. **The curse list** — one page of **Curse Cards** (9 to a page, see the Curses deck), cut
   apart and loose between the leaves. These are what a reader takes.
3. **The purified leaf** — folded, cover out: *"Open when all curses have been taken from the
   book."* Inside is the blank-page reveal and the space for the player's question. This sits
   at the back of the book, unopened until the curses are gone.

Both leaves print and fold from the printable — two sheets, double-sided, flipped on the
short edge, each folded in half across the short edge with the cover facing out.

**Where it starts:** with the Illiterate. If it isn't in play at setup, it rides in with the
First Shipment.

---

## Running it

- **The date.** Cindralok's letter is dated *exactly five years before the day you play* — the
  vengeance is due today. The printable fills the date in by itself; if you print ahead of
  time, add `?gameday=YYYY-MM-DD` to its URL so it counts back from the right day.
- **Taking a curse.** When someone takes a curse from the book, they **pick and choose** it.
  No one is stuck with a curse they don't want. Players in for a challenge should take one at
  random.
- **How long a curse lasts.** Until game end, or until it stops being fun for the player —
  whichever comes first.
- **Clearing the book.** The dread energy disperses as curses leave the book. Once every curse
  has been taken, the purified leaf may be opened: the player writes a single question and
  brings it to a storyteller, who gives a single answer.
- **Illiterate.** The owner cannot read it. Getting someone else to read it aloud is the whole
  point — and that reader is the one who gets cursed.

See the **End of Game** checklist: the tome should be cleared before the game closes.

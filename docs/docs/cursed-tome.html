Print DOUBLE-SIDED (flip on the SHORT edge) on 2 sheets. Each sheet is one leaf: the front side is the cover, the back side is the writing. Then fold each sheet in half across the short edge (horizontal crease at the middle) with the cover facing out — you get an 8.5 × 5.5 folded leaf, cover on the front, writing hidden inside. Leaf 1 (Only opened by someone who can read) goes in the front of the book prop; leaf 2 (Open when all curses have been taken) goes at the back. Tuck a page of Curse Cards (cut into 9) between them.

Leaf 1 — Cindralok's message (prints on the back side)

This tome is clearly cursed. You've never seen a more cursed object in your life. The curses are radiating off it in an almost purple glow. Reading any further will curse you. Take a curse from the book before continuing — or slam the book shut to avoid taking one.

Cindralok's Vengeance

I am Cindralok. Ancient Fire Serpent of the deep woods. Five years ago, this book was stolen from me. I have erased the knowledge contained within and replaced it with my ancient hatred.

Five years from now, if it has not been returned to me, my vengeance shall take form, and the book, its owner, their family, and every building they have ever set foot in will catch fire, and they will burn.

Dated:  

Cindralok

As curses are taken from the book, you sense the dread energy emanating from it disperses. You sense you can prevent the terrible destiny by taking every curse from the book.

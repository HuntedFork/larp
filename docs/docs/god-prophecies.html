Print DOUBLE-SIDED (flip on the SHORT edge). 4 prophecies to a page — odd pages are the covers, even pages are the matching prophecies. Cut along the dashed cross into four 4.25 × 5.5 quarters, then fold each quarter in half across the short edge (horizontal crease at its middle) with the cover facing out. You get a 4.25 × 2.75 folded card: DO NOT OPEN — <God> Prophecy on the front, the god's counsel hidden inside. There are 6 prophecies, one per god.
